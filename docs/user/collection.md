# Managing your collection

> **Available now** for manual entry, camera scanning, storage locations, conditions and
> finishes. Valuation is still to come; that section is marked below.

## Physical and digital, kept separate

The app distinguishes the cards you own **physically** from those in a **digital** collection. You
can decide, deck by deck, which of the two the optimizer works from.

The digital collection stays empty for now: importing from MTG Arena is not possible, and
[the FAQ explains why](faq.md#why-cant-i-import-my-mtg-arena-collection).

## Knowing where your cards are

Each copy can carry a **storage location**: a container, optionally a section and a position. For
example "Binder 3, page 12".

The location you type is **kept between cards**, so entering a binder means setting it once and
then adding card after card. Containers you have already used are offered as suggestions, both
here and when scanning — see [Scanning your cards](scanning.md), which can drop a whole batch
straight into a named box.

After that, "where is my Rhystic Study?" becomes a simple search.

## Per-copy information

Two copies of the same card can differ:

- **Set** and collector number
- **Language**
- **Foil** or not
- **Condition** — Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played

This information feeds valuation and trading.

## Duplicates and dormant cards

The app flags your duplicates, along with cards that appear in none of your decks. Those are the
natural candidates for trading or selling.

## Backup and transfer

At the bottom of the collection screen. **Save a backup** writes one file holding your
collection, your decks and your game log — everything you created, and nothing that can be
downloaded again. Card data is left out on purpose: it rebuilds from Scryfall in minutes, and a
26 MB file is one people stop bothering to save.

This matters most for the game log, which cannot be reconstructed from anything. Nobody
remembers last March's games.

**Restore from a file** reads one back. It refuses when the app already holds something, and the
message says what is in the way: importing *adds to* what is there rather than replacing it, so
the same cards would count twice. Save a backup of what is here first if you want to keep it.

Moving between a PC and a phone works the same way. There is no server and no account — see
[the FAQ](faq.md) — so a file is the transfer.

## Valuation

An estimate of your collection's value based on Scryfall prices, tracked over time. This part
is **not built yet**.
