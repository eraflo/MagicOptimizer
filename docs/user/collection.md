# Managing your collection

> **Available now** for manual entry, storage locations, conditions and finishes. Scanning
> arrives in phase 6 and valuation in phase 9; those sections are marked below.

## Physical and digital, kept separate

The app distinguishes the cards you own **physically** from those in a **digital** collection. You
can decide, deck by deck, which of the two the optimizer works from.

The digital collection stays empty for now: importing from MTG Arena is not possible, and
[the FAQ explains why](faq.md#why-cant-i-import-my-mtg-arena-collection).

## Knowing where your cards are

Each copy can carry a **storage location**: a container, optionally a section and a position. For
example "Binder 3, page 12".

The location you type is **kept between cards**, so entering a binder means setting it once and
then adding card after card. Containers you have already used are offered as suggestions. Once
scanning arrives in phase 6 the position will also increment on its own as you turn pages.

After that, "where is my Rhystic Study?" becomes a simple search.

## Per-copy information

Two copies of the same card can differ:

- **Set** and collector number
- **Language**
- **Foil** or not
- **Condition** — Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played

This information feeds valuation and trading.

## Duplicates and dormant cards

The app flags your duplicates, along with cards that appear in none of your decks. Those are the
natural candidates for trading or selling.

## Valuation

An estimate of your collection's value based on Scryfall prices, tracked over time. This part
arrives in phase 9.
