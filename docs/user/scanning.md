# Scanning your cards

> **🚧 Not available yet.** Shipped in phase 6. The advice below describes the intended behavior
> and will be adjusted once recognition has been measured against real cards.

## How it works

You pass your cards one at a time in front of the phone's camera, without pressing anything. The
app detects them, identifies them and adds them continuously.

Recognition works on the **artwork**, not the text. The direct consequence is that the card's
language does not matter. Your French, Japanese or German cards are recognized exactly like
English ones.

## The four destinations

Before starting a session, you choose where the scanned cards go.

| Destination | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Entering or extending your collection. A storage location can be attached automatically. |
| **New deck** | Digitizing a physical deck you already built, to analyze or optimize it. |
| **Draft or sealed pool** | Entering your pool **once the draft is over**, then building your 40 cards. |
| **Trade list** | Building a list of cards to trade, with their value. |

There is deliberately **no assistance during a draft**: electronic devices are banned in tournament
play, and it would be poorly received regardless. The pool is entered afterwards.

## Getting good results

This section will be confirmed by real measurements, but the expected principles are:

- **A plain, contrasting background.** Detection looks for the card's rectangle, so a cluttered
  background gets in the way. A plain dark table works well.
- **Diffuse lighting.** The main enemy is glare, particularly on foils. Avoid lighting aimed
  straight down the camera's axis.
- **The whole card in frame**, flat, with no finger covering the artwork. A slight tilt is fine:
  perspective is corrected automatically.
- **Pause about half a second per card.** Identification is confirmed across several successive
  frames, which prevents mistakes — but means you cannot go too fast.

## Expected edge cases

- **Reprints with identical artwork** — the artwork cannot tell them apart. The app will offer the
  possible printings, with the most recent selected by default.
- **Foils** — the hardest case, because of glare. Change the angle if recognition hesitates.
- **Sleeved cards** — should work, glare permitting.
